## 下载vulhub

下载链接：git clone
https://github.com/vulhub/vulhub.git但太慢了，我是直接下载master分支的压缩包vulhub-master.zip，把压缩包直接放进kali，然后unzip vulhub-master.zip(解压)，然后cd vulhub-master,在目下ls查看:  
![image.png](https://image.3001.net/images/20210812/1628778052_61152e44e47dc2fc3eddd.png!small)

启用环境  
进入vulhub选择复现漏洞，如solr/CVE-2017-12629-XXE(进入solr/CVE-2017-12629-XXE  
![image.png](https://image.3001.net/images/20210812/1628778069_61152e55ebf51523d4c9b.png!small)对靶场进行编译：docker-compose build  
![image.png](https://image.3001.net/images/20210812/1628778080_61152e60700512dce1c83.png!small)运行靶场：docker-compose up -d  
![image.png](https://image.3001.net/images/20210812/1628778094_61152e6e2da5a0853d6b5.png!small)速度巨慢，那么换源，  
参考博客：https://blog.csdn.net/weinipangbai/article/details/108589571  
源的镜像感觉这个更好用一点：https://nddt8zfh.mirror.aliyuncs.com  
换完源之后可以了：

![image.png](https://image.3001.net/images/20210812/1628778125_61152e8d98f3dab0ad806.png!small)完成漏洞检测之后，移除环境：docker-compose down
