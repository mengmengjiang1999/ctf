1.打开Appscan，选择创建新的扫描![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081028_61a322045d2a5e9baa6b7.png!small)

2.选择常规扫描![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081055_61a3221f3c43c78015bab.png!small)

3.选择Appscan并下一步

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081091_61a32243b450ba0b49432.png!small)

4.填写URL

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081143_61a32277965b303496b9b.png!small)

5.选择Appscan IE浏览器

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081164_61a3228c77cdc787577e4.png!small)

6.输入账号密码并点击登录

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081208_61a322b8be9c027761f06.png!small)

7.登录完成后选择我以登录到该网站，等待完成

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081221_61a322c5a6c976a06c271.png!small)![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081248_61a322e0c626b2f74c9ec.png!small)

8.显示“已成功配置登录”后点击下一步

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081263_61a322ef165a8ff3b78d6.png!small)

9.选择“缺省值”并下一步，测试优化选择正常

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081293_61a3230d9ee1568d351ab.png!small)![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081318_61a323269b446b65fbf1f.png!small)

10.选择全面自动扫描

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081331_61a323338d8cd36594003.png!small)

11.选择“是”并创建文件夹，文件夹名称可自定义

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081362_61a323521d0d24193405d.png!small)

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081377_61a3236102a5bfbd4c6e6.png!small)

12.等待扫描完成

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081416_61a32388ca3719d66ca19.png!small)![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081425_61a323913ebb5009a4ab5.png!small)

13.点击问题，就可以看见漏洞，点击漏洞就可以看到具体信息、修订建议以及请求和响应的数据包

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081460_61a323b4e39eddbf301c9.png!small)

14.导出报告，选择报告，选择行业标准，保存报告，等待报告生成

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081474_61a323c2907e8a18a0b90.png!small)![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081485_61a323cd984669cd0d5cd.png!small)

15.报告已生成并存为PDF文件

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20211128/1638081506_61a323e2955d59f3e6b56.png!small)
