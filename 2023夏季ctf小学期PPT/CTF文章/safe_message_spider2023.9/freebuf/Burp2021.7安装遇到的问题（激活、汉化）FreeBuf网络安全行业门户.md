## **一、java的安装以及环境的配置。**

1.先下载java文件。

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20210719/1626680352_60f52c200365865d62532.png!small)

2.双击下载的文件，选择安装路径。（D:\Program Files\Java）

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20210719/1626680366_60f52c2e3e7de8c15c840.png!small)

3.配置环境变量。

配置jdk环境：  
添加系统环境变量JAVA_HOME：D:\Program Files\Java\jdk-11.0.12  
添加到Path: %JAVA_HOME%\bin。

4.验证是否安装成功。![](https://image.3001.net/images/20210719/1626680557_60f52ced7a68ebd805e55.png!small)

## 三、激活。

1.把下载的激活工具移动到burp安装的路径下。

![](https://image.3001.net/images/20210719/1626680499_60f52cb318590a08eb8c2.png!small)

2.双击jar，点击Run，burp运行。

![1626681682_60f53152aee03037182cb.png!small?1626681683163](https://image.3001.net/images/20210719/1626681682_60f53152aee03037182cb.png!small?1626681683163)

3.把jar 中的Liscense内容复制到出现的窗口中，点击下一步。

![1626681862_60f532061375190f476f9.png!small?1626681862320](https://image.3001.net/images/20210719/1626681862_60f532061375190f476f9.png!small?1626681862320)

  

4.点击NEXT,再次点击Mannal Activation（手工激活）。

![1626682422_60f534367f57c149f4216.png!small?1626682422885](https://image.3001.net/images/20210719/1626682422_60f534367f57c149f4216.png!small?1626682422885)

5.选择copy request,复制到激活工具的Activation Request中，返回Activation Response值。

![1626682059_60f532cbe5708ab109d17.png!small?1626682060278](https://image.3001.net/images/20210719/1626682059_60f532cbe5708ab109d17.png!small?1626682060278)

![1626682345_60f533e9ef86cc8cdf154.png!small?1626682346758](https://image.3001.net/images/20210719/1626682345_60f533e9ef86cc8cdf154.png!small?1626682346758)

6.把Activation Response值再复制到burp中，点击Next。

![1626682370_60f53402a4fa293d61652.png!small?1626682371063](https://image.3001.net/images/20210719/1626682370_60f53402a4fa293d61652.png!small?1626682371063)

7.激活成功。
