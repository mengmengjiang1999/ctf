An important feature of the Web3 era is the emergence of dApps or decentralized applications, which can replace traditional applications.

What are the dApp and Web3 apps?

The terms "Web3 application", "Web3 application", "decentralized application", "dApps" or "Web3 dApps" are all largely synonyms. They all refer to the fragmented, often blockchain-based applications that are part of Web3.
