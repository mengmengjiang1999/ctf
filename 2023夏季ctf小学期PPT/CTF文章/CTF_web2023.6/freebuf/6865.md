Tomcat配置SSL证书别名tomcat无法识别密钥项
2022-10-25 17:04:22

Tomcat配置SSL证书一直启动失败，主要问题如下：

java.io.IOException: Alias name tomcat does not identify a key entry

at org.apache.tomcat.util.net.jsse.JSSEUtil.getKeyManagers(JSSEUtil.java:280)

别名tomcat无法识别密钥项，是因为Tomcat配置没有设置别名，该别名默认是域名或者alias，如果没有别名的，可以让wotrus重新签发SSL证书

certificateKeyAlias="alias"

certificateKeyAlias="www.wosign.com"

配置参考如下：

<Connectorport="8443"protocol="org.apache.coyote.http11.Http11NioProtocol"

maxThreads="150"SSLEnabled="true">

<SSLHostConfig>

<CertificatecertificateKeystoreFile="conf/www.wosign.com.jks"

certificateKeystorePassword="123456"

certificateKeyAlias="www.wosign.com"

type="RSA"/>

</SSLHostConfig>

</Connector>
