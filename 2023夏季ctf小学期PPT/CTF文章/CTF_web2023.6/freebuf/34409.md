2020第二届亚洲网络安全创新国际峰会
2020-07-30 14:49:36

随着人工智能、大数据、云计算、物联网等数字化技术的发展与应用，世界日益数字化与互联化。同时我们不难发现，网络攻击每年都在持续增加中，而这恰正强调了当下网络安全在全球范围内的重要性。

从近年来中国乃至国际上各种网络安全相关法规条例的颁布可知，网络安全的重要性早已上升到国家战略层面。而对不同行业、不同规模的组织和企业来说，网络安全也是必然的热门话题。如何在严格的网络安全监管下合规运营企业本地与跨国业务，建立有效网络安全攻防机制，保障数字资产、数据、云、设备、系统、网络的安全性是我们如今需要关注的重中之重。

本次峰会将采用 「线下私享+线上直播」的方式携手国内外政府、金融、信息通信、零售、医疗保健、工业制造、汽车、互联网科技等网络安全高风险行业-约300多位安全专家领导代表共同出席现场，促进跨地域、跨行业的网络安全交流与创新。主题演讲话题将涵盖网络安全法律与合规、网络安全自动化、云计算安全、数据安全、企业网络安全、最新网络安全技术与防范手段等。通过参与本次会议，您将了解权威的网络安全法规解读、全面的网络安全行业解析，最新的网络安全技术与行业情报，并有机会与各行各业的网络安全专家领导们面对面深入交流，以帮助您更好地解决工作痛点，把握潜在商机。

会议热点话题:

  * 企业如何应对当下数字世界不断变化的网络安全环境
  * 供应链风险管理与攻击防范
  * 联网设备的安全管理与用户隐私保护
  * 5G网络中的安全风险与应对
  * APP个人信息安全与隐私保护合规治理思路
  * 跨国公司数据跨境传输与隐私安全
  * 创新数据处理技术以适应复杂的监管要求
  * 《电子商务法》与网络安全
  * 医疗与数据安全：数字医疗时代HIPPA合规与隐私数据保护
  * 企业DevSecOps实施的最佳实践
  * 制药业网络安全治理实践
  * 金融机构网络安全能力的增强与提升
  * 实现车企信息安全的实践与创新
  * ……

The 2nd Asia Cybersecurity Innovation Summit 2020

Since the entire globe is growing digitized and connected with the development and application of digital technologies such as artificial intelligence, big data, cloud computing, and Internet of Things, it is easy to find that cyber attacks are continue to increase in frequency year over year, which just underscores that cybersecurity is of greater importance than it has ever been before.

Through the promulgation of the various cybersecurity related laws and regulations in China and in the world in recent years, we can see the importance of cybersecurity has already risen to the national strategic level. To organizations and businesses of all sizes across every industry, cybersecurity is also an inevitable hot topic. How to compliantly operate local and transnational business under strict cybersecurity supervision, establish an effective cybersecurity attack and defense mechanism, and ensure the security of digital assets, data, cloud, equipment, system and network is the top priority we need to pay attention to now.

This summit is a closed-door meeting which will take place both offline sharing and live streaming. Join hands with 300 attendees from government, finance, ICT, retail, healthcare, industrial manufacturing, automotive, internet technology and other high-risk industries of cybersecurity in China and global regions, to promote cross-regional and cross-industry cybersecurity exchanges and innovation. The topics of the keynote speech will cover cybersecurity law and compliance, cybersecurity automation, cloud computing security, data security, enterprise cybersecurity, the latest cybersecurity technology and preventive measures, and so on.

Highlights:

  * Latest Updates on China Cybersecurity Law
  * The Road to Regulatory Compliance for Cybersecurity
  * How Enterprise Goes Though under the Changing Cybersecurity Environment in Current Digital World
  * Supply Chain Risk Management and Attack Prevention
  * Best Practices for Data Security in Internet Company
  * How to Better Address Cybersecurity Pain Points in OT and IIoT Environments
  * Cybersecurity Insurance Mitigates Losses from Cyber Incidents
  * Cloud Security Management and Sensitive Data Protection in e-Health Age
  * Achieving Automotive Enterprise Information Security: Practice and Innovation
  * APP Personal Information Security and Privacy Protection Compliance Governance
  * Data Cross-border Transmission and Privacy Security for Multinational Company
