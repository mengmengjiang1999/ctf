Bitdefender GravityZone 2020 第二季度重大更新，新增@Human人为风险...
2020-06-27 11:18:43

您是否知道95％的网络安全事件是人为因素造成的？

从7月7日开始，Bitdefender对GravityZone进行了更新，使其具有分析用户操作的能力，以识别对组织构成安全风险的那些行为。


人为风险分析 –新的@Human GravityZone组件–增强了当前的风险分析引擎，并使您能够全面了解实际的安全状况。我们为组织提供了一种工具，用于了解风险敞口，并阻止可能在任何级别影响它们的高级威胁：@Endpoint，@Network，@Cloud，以及现在的@Human。


-新的@Human –风险分析模块的扩展，涵盖了用户产生的风险


-新的本地EDR –EDR现在可以本地化部署


-新的勒索软件缓解模块-于7月在本地版本上引入，允许在勒索软件感染的情况下自动备份和还原文件


-新的存储插件（用于云）– EDR事件的默认存储为7天。通过此插件可以存储更长的时间事件


-改进的网络流量安全分析–我们正在改进报告功能，提升了检测能力。


Did you know that 95% of cybersecurity incidents have a human error component?

Starting July 7th, Bitdefender extends GravityZone with the capability to analyze user actions in order to identify those behaviors that pose a security risk to the organization.

The Human Risk Analytics – the new @Human GravityZone component - enhances the current risk analytics engine and enables a comprehensive view of the real security posture. We give organizations the tool to understand the risk exposure and to stop the advanced threats that might affect them at any level: endpoint, network, cloud, and now, also humans.

Join us for a session learn all about how the new features grow GravityZone into a platform that is fully capable of looking after the entire enterprise cyber space. The agenda will cover all the elements of the GravityZone July release:

- The new @Human – The extension of Risk Analytics Module to cover the user generated risks

- The new EDR on-premises – This will unlock opportunities that we could not leverage because customers/prospects were looking for a fully on-premises EDR deployment

- The new Ransomware Mitigation module – Introduced on the on-premise version in July, allows files to be automatically backed-up and restored in case of ransomware infections

- The new Storage add-on (for cloud) – default storage for EDR events become 7 days. Longer time events storage available through this add-on

- The improved NTSA – We are improving reporting and detection gets a boost.
