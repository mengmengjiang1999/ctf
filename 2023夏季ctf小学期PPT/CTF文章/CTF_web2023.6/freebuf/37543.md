探测局域网存活网站Python脚本

进入一局域网,快速探测存活的网站脚本(Python):
简单思路,大佬勿喷.
`import requests

urls = [
f'http://10.2.5.{i}' #可根据具体网段更改ip
for i in range(1,256)
]
for url in urls:
try:
r = requests.get(url=url)
except:
continue
else:
if r.status_code == 200:
print(f"该ip可访问 :{url}")
else:
print(f"该ip不可访问:{url}")`
