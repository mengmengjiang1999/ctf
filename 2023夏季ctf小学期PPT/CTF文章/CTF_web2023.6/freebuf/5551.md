延期公告 | 第五届“强网”拟态防御国际精英挑战赛
2022-11-09 17:59:17

​​公 告

致各位参赛选手：

第五届“强网”拟态防御国际精英挑战赛自启动以来，一直受到全国乃至世界各地的广泛关注，线上预选赛也得到了国内高校、知名企业、CTF爱好者的积极响应。

但鉴于当前国内疫情形势，为全力配合和落实国家及地方政府疫情防控要求，切实保护全体涉赛人员的健康安全。经大赛组委会研究决定，原定于2022年11月24日-27日在南京举办的第五届“强网”拟态防御国际精英挑战赛延期举办，具体比赛时间待定，以主办方正式公告时间为准。

因赛事延期给各位选手造成的不便，我们深表歉意！非常感谢大家的理解与支持，大赛组委会将持续做好相关准备工作。期待早日重聚紫金之巅，重启巅峰之战！

第五届“强网”拟态防御国际

精英挑战赛组委会

2022年11月9日

Notice

To all contestants:

Since the launch of the THE 5th “QIANGWANG”INTERNATIONAL ELITE CHALLENGE ON CYBER MIMIC DEFENSE, it has been widely concerned by the whole country and even around the world. Qualification test has also been actively responded by domestic universities, well-known enterprises and CTF fans.

However, in view of the current domestic epidemic situation, in order to fully cooperate with and implement the epidemic prevention and control requirements of the national and local governments, and effectively protect the health and safety of all personnel involved in the event, The organizing committee of the competition has decided to postpone the 5th " QIANGWANG " Imitative Defense International Elite Challenge, which was originally scheduled to be held in Nanjing from November 24 to 27, 2022. The specific time of the competition is to be determined, subject to the official announcement of the sponsor.

We apologize for the inconvenience caused by the delay of the event! Thank you very much for your understanding and support. The organizing committee of the competition will continue to make relevant preparations. We look forward to reuniting with Zijin Peak and restarting the peak war!

THE 5th “QIANGWANG”INTERNATIONAL ELITE CHALLENGE ON CYBER MIMIC DEFENSE ORANIZING COMMITTEE

November 9, 2022​​​​
