关于(ISC)²北京分会

(ISC)²是推出信息安全领域金牌认证CISSP的美国非盈利教育组织，在中国的(ISC)2北京分会成立于2014年11月1日，专注信息安全，旨在建立北京及周边地区的一个安全技术交流的网络，促进分会会员之间的信息分享、经验交流、技术讨论和个人职业发展。

(ISC)²北京分会微信公众号：ISC2BJ

活动时间

2016年10月11日（周二）晚上18:30-20:30

活动地点

北京饭店莱佛士（会议室待通知）


演讲嘉宾

Alexander Trewby（谷歌）


演讲嘉宾介绍：

Alexander
Trewby works for Google and is responsible for Enterprise
Wearables. Before Google, he co-founded Divide, a mobile securiy
startup which was acquired by Google in May, 2014. Prior to that,
Alexander spent 10 years working for Morgan Stanley in New York
where he was Vice President of Mobile. Alexander holds a B.S. in
Economics from City University and currently lives with his son in
London.


演讲议题

Mobility and Wearable Device Security and Management


议题介绍：

It will cover the following areas:

  * Enterprise mobility in the West

  * Management technology

  * Device management

  * Application management

  * Identity and access management

  * Content management

  * Containment

  * Wearable devices and the future of enterprise mobile management
