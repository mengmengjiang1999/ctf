公司简介

厦门ABB振威电器设备有限公司成立于2009年12月，由全球领先的电力、自动化技术集团ABB和厦门振威安全技术发展有限公司合资成立。

ABB全球智能楼宇对讲系统研发和制造基地落户厦门，并快速响应市场需求推出了领先的智能楼宇对讲系统。2014年ABB振威与德国ABB公司一起同步推出了“i-家”和“free@home”两款智能家居系统。来自ABB自主研发的智能家居系统、全球领先的楼宇控制系统和引领行业技术标杆的i-bus一起构成了适用于智能楼宇和智慧建筑的强大的解决方案，更大程度上满足了家庭对更环保、更方便、更高品质生活的追求。

ABB振威在全国设有12大客户服务中心和60个销售服务机构。在海外60多个国家拥有销售渠道，拥有覆盖全球的强大营销和服务团队。同时, 优秀的产品也为公司赢得了众多奖项, 包括德国红点设计奖、IF设计大奖, 中国十大安防品牌、十大智能家居品牌以及中国安防百强企业等多个荣誉. ABB振威将继续以务实及创新精神，承担社会责任并不断关注可持续发展，致力于成为世界领先的楼宇对讲和智能家居系统的提供商。

ABB Genway Xiamen Electrical Equipment Co., Ltd., founded in December 2009, established by the leading power and automation technology group ABB and Xiamen Genway Safety Technology Development Co., Ltd.

Global R&D and manufacture factory of ABB group, located in Xiamen, quickly responded the market demand and developed the world leading Door Entrance System products. ABB Genway and German ABB Company launched two smart home systems together which are called i-jia and free @ home in 2014. ABB smart home system DES and i-bus technology provide a magnificent solution for the smart city and family. These solutions can satisfy the requirements of people who need more environment friendly much convenient and higher quality of life.

ABB Genway has 12 major customer service centers and 60 sales service agencies throughout the country. More than 60 overseas countries have sales channels, and have a strong marketing and service team covering the world. At the same time, excellent products have won numerous awards for the company, including the German Red Dot Design Award IF design award Top 10 security brand, Top 10 smart home products brand and Chinese security enterprises etc. ABB Genway is committed to become the world’s leading smart home and intelligent community solution provider.

Job Description

Mission Statement

软件需求分析，软件架构设计，软件进度和质量控制，保证软件符合产品的功能需求;

Responsible for requirement analysis, architecture design, project planning.

Main Accountabilities

1.根据产品功能要求，进行软件需求分析，制定软件设计规格；

Requirement analysis and software specification finalized according to product specification.

2.网络安全相关系统方案设计;

Cyber security related solution design.

3.关键技术决策

Key technology decision.

4.新技术，新平台预研

New technology and new platform research.

5.疑难问题定位解决

Resolve critical problems.

Knowledge, Skills and Experience

1.计算机相关专业本科学历以上，硕士优先；

Bachelor degree or above, Master degree preferred, majored in computer engineering.

2.丰富的网络安全设计经验

Rich experience on cyber security design.

3.熟悉Linux操作系统；

Be familiar with Linux operating system.

4.熟悉C语言程序设计；

Be familiar with C language.

5.熟悉TCP/IP网络通讯协议；

Be familiar with TCP/IP protocol.

6.熟悉网络Socket编程；

Be familiar with Socket programming.

7.5年以上软件开发经验；

5 years+ software development working experience.

8.2 年以上系统设计经验

2 years+ architecture design experience

9.沟通协调能力强；

Good at communication and coordination.
