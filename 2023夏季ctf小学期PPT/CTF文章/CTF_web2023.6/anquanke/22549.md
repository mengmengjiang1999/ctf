(ISC)2北京分会沙龙第3期——局内人看CISSP认证

阅读量71082

|

发布时间 : 2015-02-10 16:08:35

本期(ISC)2北京分会请来Jim
Molini分享他与(ISC)2的经历。Jim于1993年获得CISSP证书，是CSSLP第一人和该证书的主要架构师，他对(ISC)2的多个认证都有贡献，在(ISC)2美洲顾问委员会任职8年。Jim目前是微软云安全部门的高级经理。

在本次沙龙活动，Jim将花1小时介绍CISSP知识域在2015年新的变化、信息安全趋势对亚洲安全从业人员的影响、CISSP考试的学习策略，和提升个人专业技能的经验技巧。第2小时是互动环节，Jim将回答大家关于信息安全、云安全的开放性提问。

Jim
Molini平时在美国西雅图，这次在北京的交流机会很难得，欢迎报名！

议题介绍：

The CISSP Certification
Program: One Perspective

Later this year, the new CISSP
exam will be available in Chinese. Jim Molini was one of the
first people to receive the CISSP credential in 1993. He has
helped (ISC)2 develop several of the credentials offered
to security professionals. In this presentation, he will talk
about the new CISSP certification exam and changes to the domains
of knowledge. He will also describe changes in the field of
information security and how those changes affect security
professionals in Asia. His presentation will include
strategies for studying to take the CISSP exam and tips on
developing your professional expertise. After the
presentation he will answer your questions about information and
cloud security.

The presentation will be given
in English.

嘉宾简介：

Jim is a Senior Program Manager at
Microsoft, on the Operations Team for cloud identity services in
the Identity and Security Services Division.

He has more than 20 years experience
in the field of Information Security, including extensive
experience in system and software security, intrusion detection,
and risk management. He has worked in government and the
private sector, including time as CEO of Hyperon, Inc., VP of Data
Security at First USA Bank, and Computer Security Coordinator for
NASA¡¯s Space Shuttle Flight Software development team.
Jim has been a CISSP for more than 20 years and sat on the
America¡¯s Advisory Board at (ISC)2 for 8 years He was also a
primary architect of the CSSLP certification. He has written
and spoken internationally on the topic of Information Security
since 1989. He won the (ISC)2 President¡¯s Award in 2001 and
2010.
