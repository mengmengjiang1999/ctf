SecureHongKong 2016

People, Policy and Technology

September 02, 2016 (Friday) | Cyberport | 10:00 – 17:15 (Registration starts at 09:15)

(ISC)² Members Enjoy a Complimentary Seat and Earn Up to 7 CPE Credits


Building on the success of SecureHongKong conferences in the past four years, SecureHongKong 2016 will again gather Hong Kong’s CIO/CSO/CISOs, IT security thought leaders, top executives and renowned academics. Jointly organized by (ISC)2®, Internet Society Hong Kong (ISOC), Professional Information Security Association (PISA), Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) – Hong Kong & Macau Chapter, (ISC)2 Hong Kong Chapter and Cyberport, SecureHongKong 2016 will be held on 2 September 2016 at Cyberport.

The one-day conference will deliver discussion and thought provoking presentations on latest security and cyber security issues and challenges facing the industry. In addition, it will provide the attendees an opportunity to understand emerging technologies and how its adoption can help them mitigate the risk their organizations face.


1-Day Conference Pass:

  * Members of (ISC)² and co-organizers: FREE

  * Non-members: USD 50 / HKD400


For registration inquiries, please contact Ms. Helena Cortes at hcortes@isc2.org or call (852) 28506957
For sponsorship inquiries, please contact Mr. Peter Au at pau@isc2.org


*FREE shuttle bus service is available before and after the conference based on a first-come, first-served basis.

Departure time: Shuttle bus #01: 09:00 AM | Shuttle bus #02: 09:10 AM
